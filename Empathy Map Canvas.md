# Empathy Map Canvas

**Project:** HotelPro: Hotel Management Web Application
**Phase:** Ideation

> The personas below are drafted from the product's target users. Replace or refine them with findings from your own interviews or surveys if you have them.

## Persona 1: The Guest (Asha, 29, leisure and business traveller)

**Goal:** Book the right room for the right dates in a few minutes, from a phone.

| Quadrant | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| **Says** | "Is this room free on my dates?" · "How much will it cost in total?" · "Can I cancel if my plans change?" |
| **Thinks** | "Will my booking really be held?" · "Is this site trustworthy?" · "I do not want to call the hotel." |
| **Does** | Compares rooms by price and type · Tries several date ranges · Checks the booking status afterwards |
| **Feels** | Anxious about double bookings · Impatient with slow replies · Relieved by instant confirmation of availability |
| **Pains** | Unclear availability · Hidden or unclear totals · Having to phone to change anything |
| **Gains** | Instant availability feedback · Transparent price per night and total · Self-service cancellation |

## Persona 2: Front-desk Staff (Ravi, 34, receptionist)

**Goal:** Handle arrivals and departures smoothly without mistakes.

| Quadrant | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| **Says** | "Which bookings are pending?" · "Who arrives today?" · "Is room 202 ready?" |
| **Thinks** | "I hope nobody has double-booked this room." · "I need this list in one place." |
| **Does** | Confirms bookings · Checks guests in and out · Answers phone and walk-in enquiries |
| **Feels** | Under time pressure · Worried about errors at the desk · Satisfied when the system just works |
| **Pains** | Hunting through registers · Conflicting information · Repeating the same data entry |
| **Gains** | One dashboard of all bookings · One-click confirm, check in and check out · Automatic room status updates |

## Persona 3: The Admin (Meera, 45, hotel manager)

**Goal:** Keep inventory accurate and the business running without lost revenue.

| Quadrant | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| **Says** | "How many rooms are booked?" · "Take room 301 off sale for repairs." |
| **Thinks** | "Can I trust the numbers?" · "Is guest data protected?" |
| **Does** | Manages rooms and staff accounts · Reviews booking volume and value |
| **Feels** | Responsible for revenue and reputation · Cautious about security |
| **Pains** | Double bookings · No overview of booked value · Unclear who changed what |
| **Gains** | Rooms can be set to maintenance · Dashboard shows booked value · Role-based permissions |

## What the empathy maps taught us

1. Availability must be **instant and visible**, so live checks and a list of already-booked dates were added to the booking page.
2. Staff need **speed**, so every status change is a single button.
3. Trust matters, so prices are server-calculated and permissions are enforced per role.
